## 🚀 Quick Setup for Team Members
When you open this folder in Antigravity, simply type your member role in the chat:
- `Member 1` (Team Lead / Full-Stack Architect)
- `Member 2` (Backend & Database Developer)
- `Member 3` (Frontend & UI/UX Developer)
- `Member 4` (QA, Demo Data & Video Presentation Lead)

Antigravity will automatically read `.gemini/rules/team_roles.md` and configure your role boundaries and tasks immediately!
